Bipolar Junction Transistors

直流電流關係與特性曲線
P9

由射極電流回推 IC、IB

3.3

If the emitter current of a transistor is 8 mA and IB is 1/100 of IC, determine the levels of IC and IB.已知射極電流 8 mA，且基極電流是集極電流的 1/100，求 IC 與 IB。

  1. 三電流關係，把 IB = IC/100 代入

    IE = IC + IB = IC(1 + 1/100) = 1.01 IC

  2. 解出集極電流

    IC = IE/1.01 = 8 mA/1.01 = 7.92 mA

  3. 再代回求基極電流

    IB = IC/100 = 79.2 µA

  4. 驗算（直流 α）

    αdc = IC/IE = 7.92/8 = 0.990 ✓

答案

IC = 7.92 mA　；　IB = 79.2 µA（αdc = 0.990）

P21

由特性曲線讀 βdc、α、ICEO、ICBO

3.6

For the common-emitter characteristics of Fig. 3.14: (a) find the dc beta at an operating point of VCE = +8 V and IC = 2 mA. (b) Find the value of α corresponding to this operating point. (c) At VCE = +8 V, find the corresponding value of ICEO. (d) Calculate the approximate value of ICBO using the dc beta value obtained in (a).在圖 3.14 的共射極特性曲線上：求 (a) 工作點 VCE = 8 V、IC = 2 mA 的直流 β (b) 該點的 α (c) VCE = 8 V 時的 ICEO (d) 用 (a) 的 β 求 ICBO。

  1. (a) 在圖上找 VCE = 8 V 與 IC = 2 mA 的交點 Q，讀出它對應的 IB；該點落在 10 µA 與 20 µA 之間，約 18 µA

    βdc = IC/IB = 2 mA/18 µA = 111

  2. (b) β 與 α 的轉換

    αdc = β/(β + 1) = 111/112 = 0.991

  3. (c) ICEO 是 IB = 0 那條曲線的 IC 值（VCE = 8 V）

    ICEO ≈ 0.3 mA

  4. (d) 由 ICBO = (1 − α)ICEO

    ICBO = (1 − 0.991)(0.3 mA) = 2.7 µA

答案

βdc ≈ 111　；　α = 0.991　；　ICEO ≈ 0.3 mA　；　ICBO ≈ 2.7 µA

讀圖值會隨刻度略有差異（例如讀成 IB = 20 µA 就得到 β = 100）；考卷上把「讀到的 IB」寫出來，過程就完整。

P3

固定偏壓：求 IC、VCC、β、RB

4.3

Given the information appearing in Fig. 4.110, determine: a. IC. b. VCC. c. β. d. RB.圖 4.110 為固定偏壓電路，已知 RC = 2.2 kΩ、IB = 20 µA、VCE = 7.2 V、IE = 4 mA。

  1. a. 由節點電流求集極電流

    IC = IE − IB = 4 mA − 0.02 mA = 3.98 mA ≈ 4 mA

  2. b. 由輸出迴路：VCC = ICRC + VCE

    VCC = (3.98 mA)(2.2 kΩ) + 7.2 V = 8.76 + 7.2 = 15.96 V ≈ 16 V

  3. c. 直流電流增益

    βdc = IC/IB = 3.98 mA/20 µA = 199 ≈ 200

  4. d. 基極迴路：RB = (VCC − VBE)/IB

    RB = (15.96 − 0.7)/20 µA = 763 kΩ

答案

IC = 3.98 mA　；　VCC = 15.96 V　；　β = 199　；　RB = 763 kΩ

P7

射極偏壓：求 RC、RE、RB、VCE、VB

4.4

Given the information provided in Fig. 4.113, determine: a. RC. b. RE. c. RB. d. VCE. e. VB.圖 4.113 為射極穩定偏壓電路，已知 VCC = 12 V、IC = 2 mA、VC = 7.6 V、VE = 2.4 V、β = 80。

  1. a. RC 上的電壓除以電流

    RC = (VCC − VC)/IC = (12 − 7.6)/2 mA = 2.2 kΩ

  2. b. IE ≈ IC

    RE = VE/IE = 2.4 V/2 mA = 1.2 kΩ

  3. c. 先求 VB 與 IB

    VB = VE + 0.7 = 3.1 V　；　IB = IC/β = 2 mA/80 = 25 µA

    RB = (VCC − VB)/IB = (12 − 3.1)/25 µA = 356 kΩ

  4. d、e. 兩端電壓相減即 VCE

    VCE = VC − VE = 7.6 − 2.4 = 5.2 V　；　VB = 3.1 V

答案

RC = 2.2 kΩ　；　RE = 1.2 kΩ　；　RB = 356 kΩ　；　VCE = 5.2 V　；　VB = 3.1 V

P13

分壓偏壓：求 IC、VE、VB、R1

4.5

Given the information provided in Fig. 4.116, determine: a. IC. b. VE. c. VB. d. R1.圖 4.116 為分壓偏壓電路，已知 VCC = 18 V、RC = 4.7 kΩ、VC = 12 V、R2 = 5.6 kΩ、RE = 1.2 kΩ。

  1. a. RC 上 18 − 12 = 6 V

    IC = (VCC − VC)/RC = 6 V/4.7 kΩ = 1.28 mA

  2. b. IE ≈ IC

    VE = IERE = (1.28 mA)(1.2 kΩ) = 1.54 V

  3. c. 基極比射極高 0.7 V

    VB = VE + VBE = 1.54 + 0.7 = 2.24 V

  4. d. 先算 R2 的電流；因 IB 遠小於分壓器電流，取 IR1 ≈ IR2

    IR2 = VB/R2 = 2.24/5.6 kΩ = 0.4 mA

    R1 = (VCC − VB)/IR1 = (18 − 2.24)/0.4 mA = 39.4 kΩ

答案

IC = 1.28 mA　；　VE = 1.54 V　；　VB = 2.24 V　；　R1 = 39.4 kΩ（取 39 kΩ 標準值）

此題未給 β，故 R1 採分壓器近似（IB 忽略）；若要精確解，需先知道 β 才能定出 IB。

P19

分壓偏壓設計：由 Q 點反推 RC、RE、R2

4.5

a. Using the characteristics of Fig. 4.111, determine RC and RE for a voltage-divider network having a Q-point of ICQ = 5 mA and VCEQ = 8 V. Use VCC = 24 V and RC = 3RE. b. Find VE. c. Determine VB. d. Find R2 if R1 = 24 kΩ assuming βRE > 10R2. e. Calculate β at the Q-point. f. Test Eq. (4.33), and note whether the assumption of part (d) is correct.設計一個分壓偏壓網路：Q 點 ICQ = 5 mA、VCEQ = 8 V，VCC = 24 V，且 RC = 3RE。

  1. a. 直流負載線：RC + RE = 4RE

    VCC = IC(RC + RE) + VCE

    24 = (5 mA)(4RE) + 8　→　RE = 16 V/20 mA = 0.8 kΩ　；　RC = 3RE = 2.4 kΩ

  2. b. 射極電壓

    VE = IERE ≈ (5 mA)(0.8 kΩ) = 4 V

  3. c. 基極電壓

    VB = VE + 0.7 = 4.7 V

  4. d. 近似分壓公式（因假設 βRE > 10R2，IB 可忽略）

    VB = R2VCC/(R1 + R2)　→　4.7 = 24R2/(24 k + R2)

    R2 = 5.84 kΩ（取 5.6 kΩ 標準值）

  5. e. 由圖 4.111 在 Q(5 mA, 8 V) 讀出 IB ≈ 38.5 µA

    β = IC/IB = 5 mA/38.5 µA = 129.8 ≈ 130

  6. f. 檢驗 Eq. (4.33)：βRE > 10R2 ?

    (129.8)(0.8 kΩ) = 103.8 kΩ　>　10(5.84 kΩ) = 58.4 kΩ ✓　假設成立

答案

RE = 0.8 kΩ　；　RC = 2.4 kΩ　；　VE = 4 V　；　VB = 4.7 V　；　R2 = 5.84 kΩ　；　β ≈ 130（假設成立）

P5

共基極：Zi、Vo、Av、Zo、Ai、Ib

5.4

For the common-base configuration of Fig. 5.18, an ac signal of 10 mV is applied, resulting in an ac emitter current of 0.5 mA. If α = 0.980, determine: a. Zi. b. Vo if RL = 1.2 kΩ. c. Av = Vo/Vi. d. Zo with ro = ∞ Ω. e. Ai = Io/Ii. f. Ib.共基極組態：Vi = 10 mV，交流射極電流 0.5 mA，α = 0.98，RL = 1.2 kΩ。本題直接由定義求解，不需先做直流分析。

  1. a. 輸入阻抗＝輸入電壓除以輸入電流

    Zi = Vi/Ii = 10 mV/0.5 mA = 20 Ω　（即 re）

  2. b. 集極電流 = αIe，再乘上負載

    Ic = αIe = 0.98 × 0.5 mA = 0.49 mA

    Vo = IcRL = (0.49 mA)(1.2 kΩ) = 0.588 V

  3. c. 電壓增益

    Av = Vo/Vi = 0.588 V/10 mV = 58.8　（= αRL/re）

  4. d. 輸出端沒有 RC、ro 又視為無限大

    Zo = ∞ Ω

  5. e. 電流增益

    Ai = Io/Ii = α = 0.98

  6. f. 基極電流＝射極電流扣掉集極電流

    Ib = Ie − Ic = 0.5 − 0.49 = 0.01 mA = 10 µA

答案

Zi = 20 Ω　；　Vo = 0.588 V　；　Av = 58.8　；　Zo = ∞　；　Ai = 0.98　；　Ib = 10 µA

P9

固定偏壓 CE：Zi、Zo、Av（含 ro）

5.5

For the network of Fig. 5.150: a. Determine Zi and Zo. b. Find Av. c. Repeat part (a) with ro = 20 kΩ. d. Repeat part (b) with ro = 20 kΩ.圖 5.150：VCC = 12 V、RB = 220 kΩ、RC = 2.2 kΩ、β = 60、ro = 40 kΩ。

  1. 1. 直流分析（耦合電容視為開路）

    IB = (VCC − VBE)/RB = (12 − 0.7)/220 kΩ = 51.4 µA

    IE = (β + 1)IB = 61 × 51.4 µA = 3.13 mA

  2. 2. 交流射極電阻

    re = 26 mV/IE = 26 mV/3.13 mA = 8.30 Ω

  3. 3. a. 輸入阻抗＝RB 並聯 βre

    Zi = RB ∥ βre = 220 kΩ ∥ (60 × 8.30 = 498 Ω) = 497 Ω

  4. 4. a. 輸出阻抗：ro = 40 kΩ ≥ 10RC = 22 kΩ，可忽略 ro

    Zo ≈ RC = 2.2 kΩ

  5. 5. b. 電壓增益（射極接地，無 RE 分壓）

    Av = −RC/re = −2.2 kΩ/8.30 Ω = −265

  6. 6. c、d. ro = 20 kΩ < 22 kΩ，必須計入

    Zo = RC ∥ ro = 2.2 k ∥ 20 k = 1.98 kΩ

    Av = −(RC ∥ ro)/re = −1.98 kΩ/8.30 Ω = −239

答案

Zi = 497 Ω　；　Zo = 2.2 kΩ　；　Av = −265　；　(ro = 20 kΩ) Zo = 1.98 kΩ、Av = −239

判準：ro ≥ 10RC 時輸出端可把 ro 當開路，否則要與 RC 並聯。

P11

固定偏壓 CE：IB、IC、re、Zi、Zo、Av

5.5

For the network of Fig. 5.152: a. Calculate IB, IC, and re. b. Determine Zi and Zo. c. Calculate Av. d. Determine the effect of ro = 30 kΩ on Av.圖 5.152：VCC = 10 V、RC = 4.3 kΩ、RB = 390 kΩ、β = 100、ro = 60 kΩ。

  1. a. 基極迴路

    IB = (10 − 0.7)/390 kΩ = 23.8 µA

  2. a. 電流與 re

    IC = βIB = 100 × 23.8 µA = 2.38 mA　；　IE = 101IB = 2.41 mA

    re = 26 mV/2.41 mA = 10.8 Ω

  3. b. 輸入阻抗

    Zi = RB ∥ βre = 390 kΩ ∥ 1.08 kΩ = 1.08 kΩ

  4. b. 輸出阻抗：ro = 60 kΩ ≥ 10RC = 43 kΩ

    Zo ≈ RC = 4.3 kΩ

  5. c. 電壓增益

    Av = −RC/re = −4.3 kΩ/10.8 Ω = −398

  6. d. ro = 30 kΩ < 43 kΩ，改與 RC 並聯

    RC ∥ ro = 4.3 k ∥ 30 k = 3.76 kΩ

    Av = −3.76 kΩ/10.8 Ω = −348（增益下降約 13%）

答案

IB = 23.8 µA　；　IC = 2.38 mA　；　re = 10.8 Ω　；　Zi = 1.08 kΩ　；　Zo = 4.3 kΩ　；　Av = −398（ro = 30 kΩ 時 −348）

P13

分壓偏壓 CE：由 Av 反求 VCC

5.6

Determine VCC for the network of Fig. 5.154 if Av = −160 and ro = 100 kΩ.圖 5.154：R1 = 82 kΩ、R2 = 5.6 kΩ、RC = 3.3 kΩ、RE = 1 kΩ（CE 旁路）、β = 100。已知 Av = −160、ro = 100 kΩ，反求 VCC。

  1. 1. 先檢驗 ro 是否可忽略：ro = 100 kΩ ≥ 10RC = 33 kΩ ✓

    Av = −RC/re（ro 不計）

  2. 2. 由增益反推 re

    re = RC/|Av| = 3.3 kΩ/160 = 20.63 Ω

  3. 3. 由 re 反推射極電流與電壓

    IE = 26 mV/re = 26 mV/20.63 Ω = 1.261 mA

    VE = IERE = (1.261 mA)(1 kΩ) = 1.261 V

  4. 4. 基極電壓

    VB = VE + 0.7 = 1.961 V

  5. 5. 分壓公式（檢查 βRE = 100 kΩ > 10R2 = 56 kΩ，近似法可用）

    VB = [R2/(R1 + R2)]VCC

    1.961 = [5.6 k/87.6 k]VCC　→　VCC = 30.7 V

答案

VCC ≈ 30.7 V（≈ 31 V）

若把 ro 也算進去（Av = −(RC∥ro)/re），答案會是 31.3 V；因 100 kΩ ≥ 10RC，課本標準解法忽略 ro。
